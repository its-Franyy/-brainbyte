Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

At BrainByte, we care about the privacy of our developer community. This document outlines how we collect, store, share, and protect your information when utilizing our technology learning platform.

1. Information We Collect

When registering an account, subscribing to Pro pricing, or completing verified instructor applications, we collect personal identity parameters including your full name, email address, phone number, professional experience, and profiles links (such as LinkedIn or GitHub). We also store progress coordinates such as course enrollment lists, learning timings, exam performance records, and issued credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your data to deliver the educational services, verify credentials, generate on-chain certificates, distribute course updates, and manage secure billing cycles. We do not sell or trade your data to third-party advertising companies.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We use session cookies and local storage tokens to preserve authentication sessions, toggle light/dark modes preference configurations, and gather aggregated page analytics views inside our library catalog.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We rely on trusted third-party providers to secure platform infrastructure, including Supabase (auth/database buckets) and Vercel hosting engines. These services process data in accordance with strict confidentiality protocols.

5. Security Measures

Your details are protected using cryptographic protocols, including Secure Socket Layers (HTTPS), database token access limits, and JWT authorization rules. While we implement standard industry procedures, no transmission over the internet is completely risk-free.
