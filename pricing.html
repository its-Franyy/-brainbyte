Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about plans or subscriptions? We've compiled quick answers.

Can I switch between monthly and annual plans?
Yes, absolutely! You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch from a monthly to an annual cycle at any point within your Account Settings. If you switch to annual, the discount will be applied immediately to your next billing cycle.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
Yes, we offer a 14-day hassle-free money-back guarantee for all Pro memberships. If you feel the premium courses or offline guides aren't a fit for your learning goals, simply drop us an email at support@brainbyte.in and we will refund you 100%, no questions asked.
How are verified on-chain certificates issued?
Upon successful completion of all curriculum tasks, tests, and labs inside a premium course, we generate a cryptographically secured certificate. This credential is verified on-chain to confirm authentication, preventing tamper and making it easy to embed on your LinkedIn profile.
Can my university or company buy group licenses?
Yes! Under our Enterprise Plan, we offer volume-based team discounts, unified LMS integration, team learning progress indexes, and single-invoice billing sheets. Please click "Contact Sales" or email our sales team at business@brainbyte.in.
